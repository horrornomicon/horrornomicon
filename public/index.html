about

Welcome to the Horrornomicon! This is a webring for those who enjoy horror-related media whether that be art, movies, books or games etc.

This is an adult-only webring that was made to help feature & connect users with similar interests while also not excluding more NSFW-centric sites.

rules

  1. Be at least 18.
  2. You must have a hand-coded website (no carrds, strawpages, rentrys etc.)
  3. Your site must have at least some content on it; it cannot be empty or a placeholder.
  4. No hate or harassment of any kind. Sites featured here may have their own individual content warnings, and it is your responsibility to navigate accordingly. If someone hosts or likes something that you do not care for, you do not have to look at it.
  5. Needless to say, you must be into horror of some type to join.
  6. We reserve the right to turn down applications or remove anyone caught breaking these rules.

code

To add the widget to your site, just copy and paste the code below exactly where you'd like it to show up. The stylesheet link goes in your <head>, and the div goes in your page body.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://horrornomicon.nekoweb.org/onionring.css">

<!--resize the widget by changing the zoom percentage here-->
<div id="horrornomicon" style="zoom: 100%">
  <script src="https://horrornomicon.nekoweb.org/onionring-widget.js"></script>
</div>
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://horrornomicon.nekoweb.org/onionring.css">

<!--resize the widget by changing the zoom percentage here-->
<div id="horrornomicon" style="zoom: 100%">
  <script src="https://horrornomicon.nekoweb.org/onionring-widget-alt.js"></script>
</div>

To customize it further, feel free to edit or override the rules from onionring.css in your own stylesheet.
