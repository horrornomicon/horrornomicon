faq

I've emailed you, but the widget says my site isn’t in the ring.

If you haven't received a response from us, then you haven't been added quite yet. If you have heard back from us and the problem persists, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R); if this doesn't solve it, please do feel free to reach out to us again via email.

I emailed you a while ago, but I still haven't heard back.

The ringmasters may just be busy, but if it's been a month or longer and you still haven't gotten a response, feel free to send another follow-up email.

Does the widget have to be on a specific page?

No, feel free to put it wherever you would like so long as it is just on your website somewhere.

What happens if my site URL etc. changes?

Just email us anytime with your old and new URLs, and we'll update your information. Sites that appear to go dead or silently disappear may be removed periodically.

What if I suspect or notice that someone in the webring may in fact be a minor?

Please contact us privately (via email), and we'll look into it. We ask that you not take matters into your own hands and do not encourage or endorse harassment of any kind.

Still have questions? Shoot us an email at thehorrornomicon@proton.me.
